Actual ORB matching and calibrated home offsets guide the quad. The default ground is a seeded 256 × 256 m gravel/soil world, generated nearby on demand without repeating chunks. Optional photographs cover a 10 m wide plane. Terrain is flat. Motion is simplified. Camera yaw, roll, and pitch affect the rendered image, with ideal synchronized telemetry. Heading turns at up to 40°/s; tilt follows horizontal velocity. Touchdown is modeled at 0.5 m camera height. Visual loss holds descent and resumes GPS horizontal guidance. Time advances with processed frames, so speed depends on your computer. No Betaflight or hardware connection.
